Give me irrefutable proof that this works now, as an artifact.

First, say in one line what you think "this" is, then stop and wait for me to confirm before doing anything else.

If what you find shows it doesn't work, stop: no artifact, and don't fix it. Tell me what failed and show the evidence.

List anything you couldn't prove, and why.
